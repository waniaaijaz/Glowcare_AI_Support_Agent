# GlowCare FAQ (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

**Q: Are GlowCare products tested on animals?**
A: No, GlowCare products are not tested on animals.

**Q: Are GlowCare products vegan?**
A: Most GlowCare products are formulated without animal-derived ingredients, but customers with strict dietary/ethical requirements should check the ingredient list of the specific product they are interested in.

**Q: Can I use multiple GlowCare products together?**
A: Yes. A common routine is: Cleanser → Serum → Moisturizer → Sunscreen (morning), or Cleanser → Serum → Moisturizer (night).

**Q: How do I know which products suit my skin type?**
A: Each product lists suitable skin types and concerns. Customers can also ask the assistant for a recommendation based on their skin type and concerns.

**Q: Do GlowCare products contain fragrance?**
A: Fragrance content varies by product. Customers with fragrance sensitivities should check the ingredient list or ask support.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: This demo does not process real payments; in a production version this would reflect the client's actual payment options.

**Q: How can I contact a human at GlowCare?**
A: Ask the assistant to "talk to a human" or request human support at any point in the conversation.

**Q: Does GlowCare offer sample sizes?**
A: Not in this demo catalog. This would be a real business decision configured per client.
