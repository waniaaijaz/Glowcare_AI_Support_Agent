# GlowCare Cancellation Policy (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

## Cancellation Window
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 2 hours of being placed, as long as the order has not yet entered processing.

## After Processing Has Started
Once an order has entered processing (typically after the 2-hour window), it cannot be cancelled through self-service. Customers must contact support, who will attempt to stop the shipment if possible. If the order has already shipped, it cannot be cancelled and the customer should use the Return Policy instead once it arrives.

## How to Cancel
Customers can request cancellation by providing their order number to support. Support will confirm whether cancellation is still possible based on order status.

## Refunds for Cancelled Orders
If a cancellation is successful before shipment, a full refund is issued automatically to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
