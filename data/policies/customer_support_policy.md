# GlowCare Customer Support Policy (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

## Support Channels
Support is available through the GlowCare chat assistant and, for escalated cases, a human support team.

## What the AI Assistant Can Help With
- Product questions, ingredients, and usage guidance
- Product recommendations based on skin type/concern
- Pricing and availability
- Shipping, return, refund, and cancellation policy questions
- Order status lookups

## When a Case Is Escalated to a Human
- The customer explicitly asks to speak to a human
- A complaint or complex issue the assistant cannot resolve
- A safety-related concern (e.g. suspected adverse reaction to a product)
- A situation where required information is unavailable or the assistant is not confident in the answer

## Response Expectations
The AI assistant responds instantly. Escalated human support cases are typically responded to within 1 business day in this demo scenario.

## What the AI Assistant Will Not Do
- Diagnose medical or dermatological conditions
- Claim a product treats, cures, or prevents any medical condition
- Guess at information it cannot verify from official sources (product data, order data, or policy documents)
