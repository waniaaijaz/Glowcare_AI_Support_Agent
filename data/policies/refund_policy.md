# GlowCare Refund Policy (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

## Eligibility
Refunds are issued once a valid return is received and inspected, or when GlowCare confirms an order will not be fulfilled (e.g. item permanently out of stock after payment).

## Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. GlowCare does not issue cash refunds.

## Refund Timing
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval. It may take an additional 3-5 business days for the refund to appear on the customer's statement, depending on their bank or card issuer.

## Partial Refunds
If only part of an order is returned, a partial refund is issued for the returned item(s) only. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a GlowCare error (wrong item sent, defective product).

## Denied Refunds
Refunds may be denied if the return does not meet the conditions in the Return Policy (e.g. opened product without a valid reason, missing proof of purchase, past the 30-day window).
