# GlowCare Return Policy (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

## Return Window
Products may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## Condition for Returns
- Unopened products in original packaging: eligible for full return.
- Opened or used products: eligible for return ONLY if the customer experienced an adverse reaction or the product arrived damaged/defective. In these cases, contact support with details before returning.
- Products without proof of purchase (order number or receipt) cannot be returned.

## Non-Returnable Items
Sale/clearance items marked "final sale" at time of purchase are not eligible for return.

## How to Start a Return
Customers should contact support with their order number and reason for return. Support will issue a return authorization and instructions.

## Refund Timing
Once a return is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
