# GlowCare Shipping Policy (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

## Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

## Shipping Methods and Timeframes
- Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days, $4.99, free on orders over $50.
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days, $12.99.

## Order Tracking
Once an order ships, the customer receives a tracking number by email. Tracking status can also be checked by providing the order number to support.

## Shipping Destinations
GlowCare currently ships within the country of operation only for this demo. International shipping is not available in this demo version.

## Delays
Delays may occur during high-demand periods (e.g. holiday sales) or due to courier issues outside GlowCare's control. Customers will be notified by email if a significant delay occurs.
